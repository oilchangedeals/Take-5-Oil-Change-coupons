How to claim a Take 5 coupon

Three steps, and the discount is applied at the counter.

  1. Open the campaign page. Tap a card above. The offer opens on Take 5's official site.
  2. Show it at the counter before your service starts. The code or barcode is on the campaign page or on your phone.
  3. For the $15 coupon, join first. Sign up by text or email on take5.com, then show the coupon on your phone for a Core, Complete, or Ultimate oil change.

About promo codes: Take 5 has no promo-code checkout, so codes from coupon aggregator sites do not work. We tested them live and each one returned "Coupon not found." The discounts that work are the campaign links above, the text or email coupon, and printable franchise coupons.

What a Take 5 oil change costs
